# Changelog

## 0.1.1

### 中文

- 修复开机时启动、Apple 家庭和天气的开关没有滑动动画的问题，所有开关的表现现在一致。
- 侧栏和内容区用不同的底色和一条细线分开。
- 去掉侧栏底部多余的“在线”文字，只留状态指示灯。

### English

- Fixed the switches for open at login, Apple Home and the weather, which jumped instead of sliding. All switches now behave the same.
- The sidebar now stands apart from the content, with its own color and a thin line between them.
- Removed the redundant "Online" label at the foot of the sidebar; its status light says it.

## 0.1.0

### 中文

- 首个版本：在 macOS 和 Windows 上查看和控制夏普 KI-SX100。
- 空气状态、室温、湿度、功率，以及过去 24 小时的曲线；Moko 的表情和天空跟着空气变。
- 八个运行模式、电源、加湿开关，操作后回读确认。
- 五项耗材剩余、累计和每日净化空气量、能耗和电费、最长 30 天的趋势。
- 室外天气和室内外对比。
- 接入 Apple 家庭：空气净化、加湿、空气质量、室温、滤网寿命。
- 托盘面板、浅色和深色外观、中文、日文、英文，应用内更新。

### English

- First release: view and control a Sharp KI-SX100 on macOS and Windows.
- Air status, temperature, humidity, power and their last 24 hours; Moko's face and sky change with the air.
- Eight operating modes, power and humidifying, each confirmed by reading it back.
- Five consumables' remaining life, air cleaned in total and per day, energy use and cost, trends over up to 30 days.
- The weather outside, compared with indoors.
- Apple Home: air purifier, humidifier, air quality, temperature and filter life.
- Tray panel, light and dark appearance, Chinese, Japanese and English, in-app updates.
